Develop the PPBF member-management database and its web/mobile interfaces using **only the member data embedded in the supplied `PPBF_Web.html` and `PPBF_Mobile.html` files** from `PPBF_Web_and_Mobile_v16(1).zip`.

The member datasets in the two HTML files were checked and are identical. The source contains **38 distinct member profiles and 50 share serials/shares**. It does **not** contain 50 distinct people. Do not describe or fabricate 50 separate people. Preserve the source's distinction:
- Distinct member profiles: **38**
- Share serials: **50** (serials 1–50)
- Total shares: **50**

Where a field is absent or empty, show “Not provided”/“—” in the UI without inventing a value. Keep raw source values available in the database.

## 2. Core implementation requirements

1. Create exactly one member record for each of the **38** profile objects in the dataset below. Use `id` as the stable source member ID.
2. Preserve every `serials` entry and its relationship to the member. The 50 serials must each map to the correct member; do not turn a multi-serial member into multiple people.
3. Preserve every member field exactly, including personal details, contact details, nominee details, bank details, shareholding, financial balances, profit/payout data, year-wise values, extra data, and ledger entries.
4. Build searchable member directory, member profile/detail page, member-wise statement, share-serial lookup, and member financial summary.
5. Make member-level figures traceable to their source fields. Do not silently calculate over or overwrite source values. Any derived value must be labelled as calculated and its formula shown.
6. Provide controlled create/edit/delete functions according to the application's authorized roles. Keep an audit trail of who changed a record, when, and the before/after values. Do not allow an edit to erase historical ledger entries.
7. Provide validation for duplicate member IDs, duplicate share serials, invalid data types, and inconsistent references. Report discrepancies for review; do not auto-correct them.
8. Protect personally identifying information. Mask sensitive identifiers and contact details in general views; show full values only to authorized roles. Enforce authentication and database security rules on the server/database, not only by hiding UI elements.
9. Web and mobile must use the same canonical database schema and synchronized records. Updates made through an authorized device must be reflected on the other device without creating duplicates.
10. Provide import/export of the exact member dataset in JSON/CSV/XLSX, with an import preview, validation report, duplicate detection, and rollback. Preserve Bengali Unicode.
11. Include full-screen responsive layouts, clear navigation, accessible labels, and English/Bengali text support. Do not omit fields merely because they are not displayed in the directory list.
12. Include a source-data completeness report showing 38 profiles, 50 unique share serials, 50 total shares, and the source summary figures stated above.

## 6. Financial and data integrity rules

- Preserve `shares`, `paid`, `due`, `total`, `own`, `profit`, `payout`, `pt`, `fine`, `finepaid`, `yrs`, `extra`, and `ledger` exactly as stored for each member.
- Do not assume definitions or formulas for fields whose meaning is not explicitly documented in the source. Display source field names or obtain an approved business definition before applying business logic.
- Do not recalculate or replace a member's `paid`, `due`, `total`, `own`, `profit`, `payout`, `pt`, `fine`, or `finepaid` based on assumptions.
- When showing aggregate totals, distinguish the source-level `glance` figures from any newly calculated figures. Reconcile only through a clearly labelled validation report.
- Keep monetary values in Bangladeshi Taka (৳) as represented in the source. Do not round or truncate stored values; formatting for display must not alter stored precision.
- Preserve dates in their original ISO strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and display them in a user-friendly format without changing the stored value.
- Keep all nested nominee, bank, and ledger structures intact.
